# Prompt: security-audit

Goal: audit a change or area for security/privacy risks in a local-first app.

## Focus areas

- Sensitive data storage (SecureStore vs SQLite/MMKV)
- Logging of sensitive data (PII, PIN, tokens)
- QR bundle contents (leakage, integrity assumptions)
- Wipe-local-data and destructive paths
